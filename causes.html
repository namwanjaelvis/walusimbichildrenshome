Our programs

Education Support

Every child is given access to quality education through school enrollment, learning materials, uniforms, and academic support. We encourage a love for learning while equipping children with the knowledge, skills, and confidence needed to build successful, independent, and productive futures.

Feeding an orphan

Nutritious, balanced meals are provided daily to orphaned children, ensuring they receive the essential nutrients needed for healthy growth and development. Consistent access to safe, wholesome food helps improve their health, strengthen their immune systems, boost energy, and support learning and overall well-being.

Mentorship

Dedicated mentors provide guidance, encouragement, and positive role models to orphaned children, helping them build confidence, develop life skills, and make wise decisions. Through regular support, personal development activities, and meaningful relationships, children are empowered to discover their potential and achieve a brighter future.

Community outreach

Community outreach initiatives extend care and support to vulnerable families through food assistance, educational support, health awareness, and child protection programs. By working closely with local communities, we promote hope, strengthen families, improve lives, and create lasting positive change for children in need.

Hands on skills

Older children receive practical training in skills such as tailoring, agriculture and baking. These hands-on opportunities prepare them for employment, self-reliance, and successful transition into independent adulthood with dignity and confidence.

Child protection

Every child is protected from abuse, neglect, exploitation, and violence through a safe, nurturing environment and strong safeguarding practices. We work closely with families, local authorities, and community leaders to ensure every child grows up with dignity, security, and hope.

Safe home

Every child deserves a safe and loving place to call home. Many orphaned and vulnerable children face homelessness, neglect, and unsafe living conditions. Supporting the Shelter Program helps provide secure accommodation, protection, stability, and care, giving children the opportunity to grow with dignity and hope.

Heath and sanitation

At Walusimbi Children's Home, we prioritize every orphaned child's physical and mental well-being through regular health check-ups, vaccinations, nutritious care, and counselling services. We are committed to helping each child heal, grow stronger, and build a healthier, brighter future.

Discipleship

As a Christian institution, we provide spiritual guidance through Bible study, prayer, and worship. These faith-based activities help children develop strong Christian values, deepen their relationship with God, strengthen emotional well-being, and grow into compassionate, hopeful, and responsible individuals.
