Impact of your generosity

What if your generosity gave a child the chance to live a dream?

At Walusimbi Children’s Home in Wakiso, Uganda, your generosity is not an abstract idea — it is flesh and blood, laughter and learning, and hope reborn in the lives of vulnerable children. Every gift of love creates ripples that reach far beyond school fees or meals. It restores childhoods, rebuilds dreams, and raises a generation of grateful, purpose-driven young people.

Many children in Uganda go to school hungry. Primary Six student Ashim was one of them. Without enough food at home, he struggled to stay focused in class. Because of people like you, that has changed. Your support provides healthy meals for children every day. Ashim says the meals he receives help him concentrate much better and keep up with his studies. Thank you for giving children like Ashim the nourishment they need to grow strong and succeed in school.

Grace – From the Streets to a Safe Home.
At 16 years old, Grace was living with her older sister, who struggles with a mental health condition. The two of them used to walk the streets, begging for money so they could eat. They had lost both parents and were surviving on their own. Since the day we brought them into our care, everything has changed. Grace now has a safe home, regular meals, and the care of her big sister under our support. She no longer begs on the streets under the hot sun. Grace regularly prays for everyone who has helped change her life. Your impact has made a huge difference for her.


Christine – No Longer Going to Bed Hungry.
At just 11 years old, Christine has already known deep sorrow. She lost both of her parents — something no child should experience. She and her grandmother often struggled to find enough food. Christine would lie in bed with an aching stomach, worrying about the next day. Through the generosity of supporters like you, Christine now receives three nutritious meals every day. She goes to bed with a full stomach, knowing that tomorrow there will be food again. Thank you for feeding children like Christine and giving them the strength to grow healthy and strong.
