Grateful Hearts At Our Home.

What Grateful Hearts Look Like Here?

At Walusimbi Children’s Home in Wakiso, Uganda, one of the most powerful things we witness is not only growth in knowledge or skills — but growth in grateful hearts. Children who have faced loss, hardship, and uncertainty arrive here and, through God’s love and your support, begin to bloom with a thankfulness that touches everyone around them.

Every morning and evening, the children gather for prayers. Their voices rise in songs of thanksgiving — not because life is perfect, but because they have a safe home, food on the table, and people who care.

• Jerremiah who is dreaming of becoming a teacher ,recently wrote in his journal:“Thank you God for school fees and books. I will study hard so I can help others like me one day.”His grateful heart fuels his determination in class.
• The younger ones light up when they receive new uniforms or scholastic materials. They don’t just wear them — they take extra care of them, folding them neatly and proudly showing them off.“Mummy, look! Now I can go to school like everyone else. God bless the people who helped!”
• Even after a simple meal of posho and beans, you will often hear spontaneous prayers:“Thank you for this food. Thank you for this home.”

How Your Support Cultivates Grateful Hearts?
When you give, you are not only meeting a practical need. You are planting seeds of hope that grow into hearts full of thanksgiving:
• Education support clears school fees so children can learn without worry and focus on becoming all God created them to be.
• Daily care ensures they have food, clothing, and medical attention — the basic things that restore dignity and spark joy.
• Mentorship teaches them to turn gratitude into action: helping younger children with homework, sharing what they have, and praying for their sponsors by name. We believe a grateful heart is a powerful testimony. These children are learning that even in difficult circumstances, they can choose thankfulness — and that thankfulness opens doors to brighter futures.

We have seen it happen. Children who once felt forgotten are now writing letters of thanks, performing well in school, and dreaming of careers that will glorify God and serve others. Your partnership is helping raise a generation marked by resilience and gratitude. Thank you for helping us raise children who know they are loved, valued, and seen by God and by His people. Because of you, their hearts are full — not just of food or books, but of hope and gratitude.
