A Home Where Every Child Belongs.

Why Every Child Deserves a Safe Place?

In the bustling outskirts of Wakiso, just outside Kampala, there is a place where vulnerable children find more than shelter — they find home, hope, and healing. At Walusimbi Children’s Home, every child who walks through our doors discovers a safe haven where they are loved as our own. We are a registered community-based orphanage rooted in faith, committed to breaking the cycle of vulnerability one child at a time. Here, abandoned, orphaned, or struggling children receive not only the essentials — food, clothing, medical care, and education — but something far deeper: the transformational love of Jesus Christ.

I once knew fear, uncertainty, and nights without a safe place to sleep. Finding a caring home changed everything. Here, I discovered protection, love, and belonging, giving me courage to heal, learn, and dream again. ...Mariam

Too many children in our communities grow up without the security of a loving family. They face hunger, uncertainty, and the heavy weight of wondering if they truly matter. At Walusimbi Children’s Home, we say clearly: You matter. You are valued. You are deeply loved as our own. Our home provides:
• A secure environment where children can play, laugh, and rest without fear
• Quality education that opens doors to a brighter future
• Medical attention and nutritious meals to help them grow strong
• Spiritual nurturing so they grow up knowing their true worth in Christ
• Mentorship and family-like care from caregivers who treat them as sons and daughters We believe that when a child feels safe, loved, and supported, they are free to dream — and to live those dreams.

Stories from Our Home
We’ve watched shy, fearful little ones slowly blossom into confident, joyful children who declare, “I am a child of God!” One of our children recently shared during prayer time: “Before I came here, I didn’t know what tomorrow would bring. Now I know I have a family and a God who never leaves me.” Moments like these remind us why we do what we do. Every school fee paid, every meal served, and every hug given is an investment in a life that God has destined for greatness.

How You Can Make a Difference?
We are sustained by the generosity of friends, sponsors, and partners who believe every child deserves safety and love. Your support — whether through monthly sponsorship, a one-time gift, prayers, or simply sharing our story — directly changes lives. We are currently raising funds for school tuition, food, and daily needs. Even a small gift of love goes a long way in ensuring our children stay in school and continue to thrive.
Would you like to join us?
Together, we are raising children who will one day become the strength of their families and communities.
