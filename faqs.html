Frequiently Asked Questions

Learn more about child sponsorship and feel connected with our children.
What is Walusimbi Children’s Home and what do you do?

Walusimbi Children’s Home provides a safe, loving environment for vulnerable children who have lost parental care or cannot safely remain with their families. We provide nutritious meals, healthcare, education, emotional support, and stable accommodation, helping children grow, learn, heal, and succeed.

Can I visit the orphanage?

Yes, visitors are welcome at Walusimbi Children’s Home from Monday to Sunday. To protect the children’s safety, privacy, and daily routines, we kindly ask all visitors to schedule appointments in advance and confirm a suitable time before arriving.

How can I donate to Walusimbi Children’s Home?

You can support Walusimbi Children’s Home through one-time or monthly financial donations, mobile money, bank transfers, or in-kind gifts such as food, clothing, school supplies, and medical items. Every contribution helps provide essential care, education, housing, and daily needs. Please contact us for payment details or current priority needs.

What items do you currently need most?

Our most urgent needs include nutritious food and cooking essentials, school uniforms, books and stationery, clothes and shoes for children aged 2–15, hygiene and medical supplies, as well as bedding and household items. Your support in providing these essentials helps us meet the children’s daily needs and maintain a safe, comfortable home.

Can I sponsor a child?

Yes. Child sponsorship is one of the most meaningful ways to support a child at Walusimbi Children’s Home. Sponsorship helps provide essential needs such as education, nutritious meals, medical care, clothing, and daily support, giving a child greater stability, care, and opportunities to grow.

Can I volunteer at the home?

Yes, we welcome volunteers who are willing to support the children through teaching, mentoring, sports, creative activities, maintenance, or daily care, depending on their skills and our needs. Because the children live at the home, volunteers must follow our safeguarding, safety, privacy, and conduct guidelines at all times.

How do children come to live at Walusimbi Children’s Home?

Children come to live at Walusimbi Children’s Home through referrals from local authorities, community leaders, or families facing serious difficulties. We admit children who genuinely need temporary or longer-term care and are aged 2–15 years. Each admission is carefully assessed, prioritizing the child’s safety, wellbeing, and best interests.

Can the children go to school?

Yes. Education is a core part of our care at Walusimbi Children’s Home. All school-age children attend school, with support for school fees, uniforms, books, and essential learning materials. This enables them to focus on their studies, develop their potential, and build a brighter future.

How do you take care of the children’s health?

We prioritize the health and well-being of every child by providing regular medical care, routine check-ups, treatment when they are unwell, vaccinations, and preventive support. When necessary, children are referred to nearby health facilities for further care, ensuring their health needs are addressed promptly and responsibly.
