Why a warm meal is essential to a child?

At our Home, we believe every child deserves more than a roof over their head. They deserve the chance to grow strong, learn with focus, and dream free from the constant ache of hunger. Our feeding program is one of the most vital parts of the care we provide to the 29 vulnerable children (aged 2–15) who live with us. These are orphaned, vulnerable ,abandoned, displaced, or poverty-stricken little ones who now call this place family. For many of them, the meals they receive here are the most reliable and nutritious food they get each day.

Before joining the care center, I often went hungry and struggled to concentrate. Receiving regular nutritious meals gave me strength, confidence, and hope. Today, I can learn, play, and dream. ...Terry

Hunger does more than empty a child’s stomach — it empties their potential. It dulls concentration in class. It weakens the immune system. It steals the joy of play and the energy needed to learn new skills. But when a child sits down to a warm, wholesome meal, something beautiful happens. Eyes light up. Laughter grows louder. Attention sharpens in school. Bodies, minds, and spirits begin to flourish. Our feeding program is far more than “just a meal.” It is a daily act of love that quietly says: You are seen. You are valued. You matter to God and to us.

A Day of Meals at Our Home.
Whenever resources allow, we provide three balanced meals:
• Breakfast — A nourishing porridge made with maize flour, often served with bread, to give the children steady energy for the morning ahead.
• Lunch & Dinner — Staples such as posho (maize meal), rice, or matooke, paired with beans, vegetables, and small portions of protein (meat whenever possible).
The older children joyfully help in the kitchen — learning to prepare food, serve with love, and develop practical life skills that will serve them for years. Watching their hands work together with big hearts is one of our favourite sights. We also extend this care beyond our walls through community feeding initiatives, reaching children who face daily hunger on the streets or in struggling families.

The Challenge We Face Every Month.
Providing consistent, nutritious meals for 28 growing children — plus occasional outreach — remains our greatest ongoing need. Rent, school fees, medical care, and clothing all compete for every shilling. Many months we stretch what we have, trusting God and the generosity of friends like you to close the gap. Every plate of food represents answered prayers and faithful support from people who believe in second chances. Together, we are not just feeding bodies. We are nourishing hope, restoring dignity, and giving children the strength to dream again.
