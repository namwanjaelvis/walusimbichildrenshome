Our Events

Sports and Soccer

Our boys love soccer — and we love what it teaches them. Through training and matches, they build discipline, teamwork, confidence, and resilience. The field becomes a place where they support one another, overcome challenges, and experience the pure joy of play.

Donate

Children's Day

Children’s Day is a special opportunity to celebrate every child with love, joy, and encouragement. Through games, music, dancing, gifts, and shared meals, children enjoy a memorable day while feeling valued, supported, and reminded that their dreams and happiness truly matter.

Donate

Back to school

The Back-to-School Event prepares children for a successful academic year by providing essential school supplies, uniforms, learning materials, and encouragement. It is a meaningful opportunity to motivate children, celebrate education, and remind them that learning can open doors to brighter and independent futures.

Donate

Community

Community Outreach Day brings together children, families, volunteers, and local supporters to promote care and compassion. Through food support, health awareness, child protection activities, and community engagement, we strengthen relationships and extend hope to vulnerable children and families who need support.

Donate

Fundraising

Our fundraising and charity events bring compassionate supporters together to make a meaningful difference in children’s lives. Contributions help provide food, education, healthcare, shelter, clothing, and other essential needs, ensuring vulnerable children receive the care, opportunities, and support they deserve to thrive.

Donate

Christmas Outreach

Our Christmas celebration brings children, caregivers, supporters, and community members together to share joy and gratitude. Through worship, music, games, gifts, and a special meal, we create beautiful memories while reminding every child that they are loved, valued, and never forgotten.
