WHO WE ARE

4 Years of impact

Walusimbi Children’s Home is a Christian family-based care home providing a safe, loving, and nurturing environment for vulnerable and orphaned children. We currently care for 30 children, offering shelter, nutritious food, education, healthcare, spiritual guidance, and emotional support. Many children have experienced loss, abandonment, poverty, abuse, neglect, or life on the streets. After appropriate assessment and approval by relevant authorities, each child is welcomed into our caring family.

It is natural for us as parents and caregivers to want our children to grow up in a healthy and safe environment. Yet it is equally important that we pause from time to time and ask ourselves: What do our children think of us? We carry the responsibility of correcting their faults with love, celebrating every moment they are helpful or hardworking and encouraging the good actions that grow into lasting habits. Above all, we teach them the love of Jesus Christ-the foundation that shapes who they become.

Mission

To provide a stable, loving family home for vulnerable and orphaned children where they receive quality education, nutritious food, healthcare, clothing, and Godly principles — restoring their dignity, giving them hope, and empowering them to become future leaders who will transform their communities, Africa, and the world.

VISION

We believe every child is precious to God and holds the potential to become a great African leader. Our vision is to raise children who are not only cared for but fully empowered — equipped with education, strong moral values, and Godly principles — so they can one day become the next generation of African leaders and change-makers who will impact the world.

Compassion

We cannot sit back and watch these children suffer alone. Driven by compassion and the conviction that every child deserves hope, we provide a complete home: nutritious food, quality education, healthcare and medication, clothing, and above all, the love and spiritual guidance that helps restore their dignity and future. Through our Orphanage Ministry, we offer more than shelter — we offer healing, belonging, and the chance to dream again.

Community

At Walusimbi Children’s Home we intentionally raise children within a true family and community environment. Living together as brothers and sisters under the care of a mother figure creates the stable, nurturing atmosphere where proper morals, respect, and life skills are naturally learned. This family model forms the foundation of everything we do, preparing our children not only to thrive personally but also to contribute positively to the wider community and society.

What is child sponsorship?

Child sponsorship means encouraging a child and donating to them monthly. This consistent support empowers a local church to care for a child's immediate needs, introduce them to the gospel and prepare them for a brighter future beyond poverty.

What does child sponsorship provide?

Child sponsorship introduces children to Jesus, showing them the truth of the good news in action through critical care. Child sponsorship equips a child with education, medical care, safe community, protection from abuse, nutrition support, safe home and a voice of their own.
