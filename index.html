ENDLESS HOPE

MOTHER OF OUR HOME

Madam Harriet is a devoted Christian caretaker whose life reflects compassion, service, and unconditional love. Though she never had children of her own, she has lovingly embraced many children as her own. She prepares their meals, guides their character, offers wisdom and encouragement, and provides a safe, nurturing presence. The children affectionately call her Jjajja, honoring her as a cherished grandmother whose love continues to shape their lives.

Discipleship

Our program helps vulnerable children develop faith, character, hope, and a strong sense of purpose. Through Bible teaching, prayer, mentorship, and practical guidance, children learn values such as love, honesty, compassion, and responsibility. This spiritual foundation provides encouragement, emotional strength, and positive direction as they grow into responsible adults.

Every child deserves a safe, loving family environment where they feel valued and protected. Our girls

Every girl deserves safety, dignity, education, healthcare, and the confidence to dream. Vulnerable girls may face greater risks of neglect, abuse, and limited opportunities. Our support provides a safe environment, essential needs, guidance, and encouragement, helping girls grow with confidence, discover their potential, and build brighter, independent futures.
